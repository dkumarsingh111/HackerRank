## Senior Technical Project Manager
Department: Engineering & Technology
Reports To: Director of Program Management
Location: Hybrid / Remote Options Available

## Job Summary
We are seeking a delivery-focused Senior Technical Project Manager to lead cross-functional teams in developing, launching, and scaling our core enterprise software products and cloud infrastructure. In this role, you will bridge the gap between technical execution and business strategy, managing the complete project lifecycle from initial discovery through deployment. You will be responsible for mapping dependencies, mitigating risks, and ensuring technical teams (Software Engineering, DevOps, and QA) deliver high-quality solutions on time and within scope.


## Key Responsibilities

* Project Execution: Lead end-to-end delivery of complex software development, system integrations, and cloud migration initiatives.
* Agile Facilitation: Act as Scrum Master for multi-disciplinary sprint teams. Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, backlog grooming, and retrospectives.
* Stakeholder Management: Serve as the primary liaison between technical staff, product managers, and executive leadership to align engineering output with business objectives.
* Resource & Timeline Ownership: Build and maintain detailed project plans, release roadmaps, and resource allocation trackers while proactively managing scope creep.
* Risk Management: Identify technical bottlenecks, operational constraints, or security risks early. Develop and execute concrete mitigation plans.


## Requirements & Qualifications## Experience & Education

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems, or a related technical field (or equivalent professional experience).
* 5–8 years of direct experience managing software development or IT infrastructure projects in an Agile environment.
* Proven track record of managing project budgets exceeding $2M+ and leading distributed or remote teams.

## Technical Skills & Tools

* Deep understanding of software engineering principles, CI/CD pipelines, DevOps workflows, and cloud architecture (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
* Advanced proficiency in project management tools, specifically Jira, Confluence, Monday.com, or MS Project.
* Ability to interpret technical architecture diagrams and translate technical limitations into clear business impacts.

## Certifications (Preferred)

* Project Management Professional (PMP)
* Certified ScrumMaster (CSM) or Advanced Certified ScrumMaster (A-CSM)



## Core Competencies

* Problem Solving: Ability to navigate ambiguity and resolve complex technical deadlocks.
* Communication: Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
* Leadership: An empathetic, servant-leader approach to team management that fosters collaboration and continuous improvement.
